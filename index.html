COMMON QUESTIONS

A clearer way to get started

Transparent access, feature availability, and learning-first expectations.

Do I need an account to learn or practise?

No. The Academy, charts, risk calculator, and virtual simulator are open directly to guests. Sign-in is only needed for personal account features such as the dashboard and journal.

Are SIF PRO and SIF VIP available to buy?

Not yet. The displayed amounts are proposed plan prices only. PRO and VIP remain unavailable, and this site does not collect subscription payments.

Does the simulator use live prices or a broker?

No. The simulator uses manually entered practice prices and fictional funds stored in this browser. It does not connect to a broker or place real trades.

Is this platform financial advice or a signal service?

No. Lessons and tools are educational only. Forex and leveraged trading involve substantial risk; no tool, lesson, or result guarantees an outcome.
