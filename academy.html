Start your forex journey by understanding the fundamentals of the foreign exchange market.

What is Forex?

Forex means Foreign Exchange. It is the global market where currencies are exchanged against one another.

Example

EUR/USD represents the euro against the US dollar.

Important Terms

  • Currency pairs
  • Base currency
  • Quote currency
  • Spread
  • Leverage
  • Broker

Types of Currency Pairs

  • Major pairs: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY.
  • Minor pairs: EUR/GBP, EUR/JPY.
  • Exotic pairs: USD/NGN and USD/TRY.
Currency pairs show the value of one currency relative to another.

What is a Pip?

A pip is a standard unit used to measure price movement.

What is a Lot?

A lot describes the size of a forex trade.
  • Standard lot: 100,000 units.
  • Mini lot: 10,000 units.
  • Micro lot: 1,000 units.
The four commonly discussed forex trading sessions are:
  • Sydney session.
  • Tokyo session.
  • London session.
  • New York session.
Session opening times change with daylight saving time and should be checked against the relevant market calendar.

Develop your technical analysis and learn how price moves across different market conditions.

What is Market Structure?

Market structure describes how price forms swing highs and swing lows.

Uptrend

Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL).

Downtrend

Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL).

Ranging Market

Price moves between support and resistance without a clear directional trend.

Support

An area where buying interest has previously prevented or slowed a decline.

Resistance

An area where selling interest has previously prevented or slowed a rise. These are zones of interest, not guaranteed reversal points.

Three Market Conditions

  • Bullish trend: generally rising prices.
  • Bearish trend: generally falling prices.
  • Sideways market: price moving within a range.
Always consider the timeframe when analysing market direction.

Why Risk Management Matters

Risk management helps control potential losses. Important principles:
  • Know your risk before entering a trade.
  • Use an appropriate stop loss.
  • Avoid excessive leverage.
  • Do not risk money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Maintain a trading journal.

What is Liquidity?

In trading analysis, liquidity refers to areas where orders may be concentrated.

Common Liquidity Areas

  • Equal highs.
  • Equal lows.
  • Previous day high.
  • Previous day low.
  • Previous swing highs and lows.

What is BOS?

Break of Structure describes price breaking an important swing level in the direction of the existing market structure. It can provide evidence of continuation, but context and confirmation matter.

What is CHoCH?

Change of Character describes a potential shift in price behaviour when price breaks a significant swing point against the previous direction. It may indicate a possible change in market direction, but does not guarantee one.

What is FVG?

A Fair Value Gap is a three-candle price pattern that traders interpret as an imbalance in price delivery.

Types

  • Bullish FVG.
  • Bearish FVG.
Traders may study these areas as potential zones of interest.

🧠 TRADING PSYCHOLOGY

Understand the mental discipline required for responsible trading.

Discipline means following a trading plan rather than making emotional decisions. Important habits:
  • Follow your trading rules.
  • Avoid revenge trading.
  • Accept that losses are part of trading.
  • Do not chase the market.
  • Review your performance regularly.
A trading plan should contain:
  • Trading strategy.
  • Preferred currency pairs.
  • Trading sessions.
  • Entry conditions.
  • Stop-loss rules.
  • Take-profit rules.
  • Risk limits.
